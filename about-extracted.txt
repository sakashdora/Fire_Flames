ABOUT SAMT
Srusti Academy of Management & Technology (Autonomous) is an
institution built on the belief that education must evolve with the
world it serves.
We are a space where academic structure meets real-world
relevance and where students are encouraged to grow—not just
academically, but personally and professionally. At Srusti, learning
is purposeful, experiences are intentional, and outcomes matter.
We prepare students to think critically, communicate confidently,
and act responsibly—whether they choose corporate careers,
entrepreneurship, higher studies, or social impact paths.

General Guidelines
Participants must complete registration and pay the applicable fee before the event begins.
Team members must remain the same after registration unless the organising team approves a
change.
Participants must follow the event schedule, instructions and time limits announced by the
organisers.
Unfair practices, disruption, damage to property or disrespectful behaviour may lead to
disqualification.
The decision of the external evaluator/jury and the organising committee will be final. Tie-
breaks, where required, will be
decided by the evaluator.
Prize distribution is subject to completion of the event and verification of results by the
organisers.
