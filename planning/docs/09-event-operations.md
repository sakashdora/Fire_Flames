## Staffing proposal
Appoint an institution programme lead and two named super admins; one event lead per organizer; at least two distinct authorized finance reviewers to avoid self-review deadlocks; a technical lead and support deputy. Suggested campus duties: two welcome/check-in desks with two volunteers each, one help/payment-exception desk, a station lead for each active venue, dedicated marshals at every treasure checkpoint and a separate esports room lead. Actual counts depend on venue distances and attendance.

External evaluators are required by the sources for IPL, Brand War, Bazar and Exhibition; internal evaluators for trivia and treasure hunt. Assign judges explicitly, declare conflicts, test score entry and provide a documented paper fallback. A teacher can fill these roles only when assigned. Staff duties have location, time window, supervisor and acknowledgement.

## Programme preparation
Before registration opens: approve pricing modes, rules, privacy/refund terms, eligibility, capacities and official beneficiary. Before one week to event: verify venue layout, power, internet, registration throughput and accessibility. Before one day to event: freeze roster changes except supervised corrections, publish reporting instructions, prepare exception ledgers and print contact sheets for authorized supervisors.

Every morning: count staffed desks, test a valid/invalid/revoked QR on real phones, verify network and backup connectivity, review outstanding payments, confirm judges and announce changes. At close: reconcile attendance exceptions, outstanding credits, score drafts and unresolved incidents; revoke completed shifts and secure devices.

## Payment desk procedure
Verify identity and registration reference, locate the actual payment state, and direct unresolved bank-credit cases to finance. Volunteers cannot approve screenshots. Finance checks authorized bank records, records the decision and confirms the correct event/roster. If no slot remains after a late credit, escalate reallocation/refund. End-of-day report separates billed amount, verified receipts, pending evidence, unmatched credits and actual completed refunds; none are reported as interchangeable revenue figures.

## Judge and results procedure
Publish rubric and tie-break version before activity; train judges with test candidates. Judges draft scores, check them, submit and lock. Event admin reviews completeness and anomalies without silently changing judge marks. A correction requires authorized reopening, reason, judge revision and renewed lock. Admin proposes results; super admin publishes after score/tie verification. Show published revision/time. A later correction creates a new revision and notifies affected participants. Record winner identity and prize disbursement separately from the public leaderboard.

For Bazar popularity, save official poll window and verified evidence; no fake vote or web poll stand-in. For Treasure Hunt, verify checkpoint order, correctness, finish times and penalties; discipline selection uses its own rubric. For IPL and esports, do not run scoring without an approved formula.

## Disruptions and fallback
Network outage: pause online success confirmations, use numbered paper attendance records under supervisor control, and reconcile against server uniqueness constraints when restored. Power failure: protect devices, use approved backup connectivity, update reporting times and keep crowd flow safe. Missing volunteer: supervisor reassigns duty through an audited command. Safety incident: follow college emergency procedures and local trained responders; the app records incident status but does not replace campus safety authority.

Payment fraud concern: do not accuse a participant based on a screenshot; quarantine the claim for finance review. Data exposure: suspend affected access and invoke the security runbook. Weather or venue closure: programme lead approves rescheduling/cancellation, super admin publishes and notifications go to affected registrants. Refunds follow the approved policy, not ad hoc volunteer promises.

## Closure outputs
Produce per-event confirmed registrations, attendance, final results, award records, finance reconciliation, unresolved refund list, incident summary and staff handover. Remove expired permissions, archive the published programme, retain records according to approved policy and schedule deletion of unnecessary evidence. Optional certificates require confirmed participation criteria, approved signatories and a separate issuance register.
