## Required event rules
All participants register and pay before the activity; roster changes need organizer approval. Schedules, time limits, conduct and fair play apply. Disruption, cheating, disrespect or property damage can disqualify. Evaluator and committee decisions are final under the source rules. Prizes require completed activity and verified results.

IPL Auction uses an external evaluator and ranking by points. Budget, player pool, squad size, role constraints and scoring formula are not specified; publish a versioned rules supplement before play. Recognized bids are binding, overspending is prohibited and teams explain squad choices.

e-Trivia uses an internal evaluator and one point per correct entrepreneur answer. Quizmaster defines round formats, answer submission and time limits. Phones, internet and outside help are prohibited unless a round allows them. Publish whether response time affects score or only ties before the quiz.

Treasure Hunt rewards the first and second teams to solve all clues/checkpoints correctly in the least time. The discipline award is separate and discretionary, although shown in the third-prize column. Keep teams together unless a checkpoint allows separation; prohibit unsafe areas, disruption, tampering and unauthorized help. Server timestamps plus marshal records determine ordered progress; GPS is not required or proof of completion.

Brand War uses an external evaluator. Teams complete a brand task/pitch within time, use original or credited content and answer questions respectfully. Time deductions must be defined before judging and recorded, not silently subtracted.

Bazar awards Best Stall by Jury, Most Popular Stall and Innovative Stall. The official ED Cell Instagram live poll determines Most Popular; organizers announce the voting window and verify results. The platform records verified poll evidence and approval, rather than replacing it with a website poll. Stalls manage setup, accurate pricing, cleanliness, safety, equipment and clear walkways.

Exhibition entries explain the problem, solution, users, value and development stage. External jury can request demos and ask about feasibility, costs and differentiation. Teams disclose third-party work and special power/setup needs. Multiple awards for one entry are not assumed.
