## Rules and evaluation
- General: register and pay before start; roster changes need organizer approval; follow schedule/time limits; unfair practices, disruption, property damage or disrespect can disqualify; evaluator/jury and organizers have final authority; prizes depend on completion and verified results.
- IPL: external evaluator, ranked by points. Budget, squad limits, player pool and role requirements announced before start. Recognized clear bids only; confirmed bids cannot be withdrawn; no overspending; teams explain squad selections. Point formula not supplied.
- e-Trivia: internal evaluator; entrepreneur identification from clues/images/achievements/business hints; one point per correct answer. Quizmaster sets rounds, time limits and submission method. No phones/internet/outside help unless allowed for a round. Correctness first; speed may affect scoring or tie-breaks as announced.
- Treasure Hunt: internal evaluator; solve clues/checkpoints in announced order and time. First two finishers win main awards. Discipline is separate discretionary award, despite placement as third prize in tables. Teams stay together unless instructed; no unsafe/restricted areas, disrupting classes, property damage, clue tampering or unauthorized help.
- Brand War: external evaluator; brand knowledge, positioning, creative thinking and short pitch/task. Original or credited content, respectful presentations, time limits, follow-up questions and possible overtime deductions.
- Bazar: external jury for Best Stall and Innovative Stall. Most Popular determined by live official SRUSTI ED Cell Instagram poll during announced window, verified by organizers; fake/automated voting can disqualify. Teams handle setup, cleanliness, accurate pricing, necessary permissions, safe food/equipment handling and unobstructed walkways.
- Exhibition: external jury; explain problem, solution, target users, impact and development stage; own work with third-party references identified. Safe demonstrations, advance notice of power/setup needs; be ready on feasibility, costs, users, differentiation and next steps. Jury assigns appropriate award category; multiple awards not guaranteed.
