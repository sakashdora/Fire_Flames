RULES, GUIDELINES, JUDGING RUBRICS & PRIZE DETAILS
Entrepreneurship Development Cell (ED Cell)Srusti Academy of Management & Technology (Autonomous) | November 2026
This document outlines the participation rules, registration fees, judging criteria and prize details for the games, Srusti Bazar and Srusti Exhibition. Participants are expected to follow the instructions shared by the organizing team.
General Guidelines
Participants must complete registration and pay the applicable fee before the event begins.
Team members must remain the same after registration unless the organizing team approves a change.
Participants must follow the event schedule, instructions and time limits announced by the organizers.
Unfair practices, disruption, damage to property or disrespectful behavior may lead to disqualification.
The decision of the external evaluator/jury and the organizing committee will be final. Tie-breaks, where required, will be decided by the evaluator.
Prize distribution is subject to completion of the event and verification of results by the organizers.

Srusti Bazar – Stall Guidelines
Each stall team is responsible for its setup, display, cleanliness and materials.
Stalls must be ready by the reporting time communicated by the organizers and remain operational during the allotted hours.
Pricing and product/service information should be clear and accurate. Participants are responsible for any permissions or safety precautions required for their offerings.
Electrical equipment, food handling and other potentially hazardous activities must follow organizer instructions.

Award Categories



Award
What the jury will look for
Best Problem identified – ₹3,000
A clear value proposition, defined customer segment, viable business model and realistic path to market.
Best Working Prototype – ₹3,000
A functional or convincingly demonstrated prototype, sound design choices and evidence that it addresses the stated problem.
Best Scalability idea – ₹3,000
Strong originality, meaningful problem-solving potential, room for development and a compelling future opportunity.




Rules & Guidelines
Teams must present their own work and clearly identify any third-party tools, materials or references used.
The display should include the idea/problem, proposed solution, target users and current stage of development.
Working models must be demonstrated safely. Teams must inform organizers in advance about power requirements or special setup needs.
Teams should be prepared to answer questions about feasibility, costs, users, differentiation and next steps.
The jury may allocate an entry to the most appropriate award category based on the submitted work; one entry should not assume it will receive multiple awards.
